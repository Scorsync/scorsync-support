# Coverage

What the public support site covers for the Dec 5 pilot (milestone 1 of the IA approved in SCO-2053), page by page.

- **IA rows**: feature-map rows from Part 1 of the IA (`kb-research/ia-feature-map-2053.md`). "Hub" means a landing page that routes to the how-tos.
- **Shots**: screenshots on the page.
- **Checked in app**: *yes* = steps and wording seen on the bench or in the walkthroughs; *partly* = some steps come from the code only (the page's `:::review` says which); *no* = nothing seen in the app yet.
- **Reviews**: open `:::review` callouts waiting for Austin.

The For writers section (`/style-guide/`) is internal guidance and isn't listed.

## Not documented, and why

- **Later pages (second feature map), not in this pilot:**
  - Setting up a meet in Meet Builder: create a meet, equipment and days, event setup, getting entries in, divisions and age groups, sessions and flights, placing athletes and run order, Auto-assign and Auto-schedule, finals, assigned training, undo and redo, seating judges, awards setup, printing the schedule (rows 31–45, 48, 50, 53). The pilot pages assume the meet is already built.
  - After the meet: awards and the announcer screen, the judge consistency report, exporting results (118–121).
  - Your organization: inviting people and setting roles, volunteer stations and coach codes (54, 87, 88).
  - Scorsync Desktop extras: the spectators' QR code on the venue Wi-Fi, linking a computer for USAG import, updates in depth (16, 23, 30).
  - Judges and chairs: keyboard scoring, the results browser, the meet referee and inquiries, ties and rank overrides (66, 103–105, 114–117).
  - Coaches and club admins: checking a roster with a code, sharing a club's results (89, 129).
  - Troubleshooting and tips added later: judge certifications, the stream display, and the later tips (55, 128, plus the Later additions in IA section 9).
- **Out of scope (another product's help):** scorsync.com accounts, subscriptions and billing, help for parents and fans, Scorsync for iPhone, and Scorsync Live. Pages say in one line what the host's action does on scorsync.com and link to [help.scorsync.com](https://help.scorsync.com).
- **22 support-only tools kept out of the public site** (9 support-only, 13 do-not-document). Where a reader might need one, the page says to contact Scorsync support (support@scorsync.com).
