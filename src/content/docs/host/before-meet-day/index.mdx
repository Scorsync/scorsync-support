**Who it's for:** Host (meet director) · Scorsync Cloud and Scorsync Desktop

Work down this list in the week before your meet. Each line links to the full how-to.

## Before you start

- Your meet is built in Meet Builder: sessions, flights, athletes and judges are in place. See [Build your meet in Meet Builder](/host/before-meet-day/build-your-meet/).
- On Scorsync Desktop, the laptop and travel router are set up. See [Getting started with Scorsync Desktop](/offline-host/).

## A week before

1. [Rehearse your meet](/host/before-meet-day/rehearse/) with the chair and a judge on real tablets.
2. [Print judge sign-in sheets and data-entry cards](/host/before-meet-day/print-judge-sign-in-sheets/), so every judge has their sign-in on paper.

## The day before

3. [Lock the schedule](/host/before-meet-day/lock-the-schedule/) once sessions, flights and run orders are final.
4. [Print start lists and paper backup sheets](/host/before-meet-day/print-start-lists/). Do it after the lock, so they don't go out of date.
5. [Connect your meet to scorsync.com](/host/before-meet-day/connect-to-scorsync-com/), while you have internet. Skip this if your meet won't publish results.

## Morning of

6. [Get judges signed in](/scoring-table/get-judges-signed-in/) on their tablets.
7. [Go live](/host/before-meet-day/go-live/). Every score from then on is real.

:::caution
End the rehearsal before you connect to scorsync.com or go live. Both need the rehearsal to be over.
:::

## If something goes wrong

- **"Schedule locked. Unlock to edit."** For a scratch, a level or a flight change, don't unlock: use [Meet-day changes](/host/meet-day-changes/). For anything else, see [Lock the schedule](/host/before-meet-day/lock-the-schedule/).
- **Go live is greyed out.** A row on **Ready to go live?** has a red cross. Select **Fix** next to it. See [Go live](/host/before-meet-day/go-live/).
- **There's no Go live button.** You're still rehearsing. Select **End rehearsal** first.
