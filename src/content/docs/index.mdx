**Who it's for:** Everyone running a meet with Scorsync: hosts, the scoring table, judges and chairs.

## Running a meet today?

Open the runbook for your setup and work down it.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Meet-day runbook: Scorsync Cloud" href="/runbook/scorsync-cloud/" icon="ph:cloud">
    You sign in at scoring.scorsync.com in a web browser.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Meet-day runbook: Scorsync Desktop" href="/runbook/scorsync-desktop/" icon="ph:laptop">
    Scorsync is installed on the host's laptop at the venue.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Not sure which? See [Which setup do I have?](/start-here/which-setup/)

## Pick your role

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Host" href="/host/" icon="ph:calendar-check">
    You run the meet: build it, rehearse, lock the schedule, go live, and handle scratches and changes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Offline host on Scorsync Desktop" href="/offline-host/" icon="ph:wifi-high">
    You run Scorsync on your own laptop, with a travel router for the judges' tablets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Scoring table" href="/scoring-table/" icon="ph:desk">
    You sign judges in, watch the floor in Run and keep sessions moving.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Judges" href="/judges/" icon="ph:gavel">
    You score on a tablet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chair" href="/judges/chair/" icon="ph:crown-simple">
    You run a panel: sign in your judges, call up each athlete, and confirm flights official.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Something wrong?

Go to [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/) and find what you see on screen. If the tablets are down, switch to the [paper backup sheets](/host/before-meet-day/print-start-lists/).

Questions before the meet: [FAQ](/help/faq/) · [Things you might not know](/help/tips/)

Following a meet as a parent or fan? Go to [help.scorsync.com](https://help.scorsync.com).
