**Who it's for:** Offline host · Scorsync Desktop

Start here if you'll run your meet on your own laptop. Begin at least a week before the meet.

## How it fits together

| Part | What it does |
| -- | -- |
| Your laptop | Runs Scorsync Desktop: the whole scoring system and Meet Control. It works with or without internet. |
| Your travel router | Makes the scoring Wi-Fi. Only the laptop and the judges' tablets join it. Scorsync doesn't lend routers. |
| Judges' tablets | Join the scoring Wi-Fi and open the judges' address in the browser. There's nothing to install. |
| Internet (optional) | Comes into the router, only to publish results to scorsync.com. |

The judges' address is in the Scorsync menu on the laptop, after **Judges open**.

![The Scorsync menu on a Mac: Running, "Judges open" with the judges' address, a second address after "Or", and the rest of the menu](./images/tray-menu-running.png)

## Your path

1. **Get a travel router** and set up the scoring Wi-Fi. See [What to bring](/offline-host/set-up-the-venue-network/#what-to-bring) and [Set up the router](/offline-host/set-up-the-venue-network/#set-up-the-router).
2. **Install Scorsync Desktop**, while you have internet. Download it from app.scorsync.com/downloads. See [Install on a Mac](/offline-host/install-on-mac/) or [Install on Windows](/offline-host/install-on-windows/).
3. **Run first-run setup**, and link this computer while you have internet. The pilot license covers you through March 2027. See [Run first-run setup](/offline-host/first-run-setup/).
4. **Check your network** at home, then again at the venue. See [Check your network before the meet](/offline-host/check-your-network/).
5. **Connect the judges' tablets.** See [Connect the judges' tablets](/offline-host/connect-judges-tablets/).
6. **Keep the meet running**: power, sleep, and closing the window. See [Keep the meet running](/offline-host/keep-the-meet-running/).
7. **Set up backups**: the USB copy, before meet day. See [Back up your meet](/offline-host/backups/).

Then prepare the meet itself like any host: see [Before meet day](/host/before-meet-day/).

:::danger[Never Quit Scorsync during the meet]
**Quit Scorsync**, and ⌘Q on a Mac, stop scoring for every judge. Scorsync asks "Quit Scorsync?" first: select **Cancel**. Close the window instead.
:::

## If something goes wrong

- **A tablet can't open the judges' address.** Type the second address from the menu, the one after **Or**. See [Connect the judges' tablets](/offline-host/connect-judges-tablets/).
- **The "Go live works until…" date is before your meet.** Link this computer while you have internet. When the date passes, only **Go live** for a new meet stops: meets already live keep running. If linking fails, email support@scorsync.com.
