**Who it's for:** Everyone · Scorsync Cloud and Scorsync Desktop

## Before the meet

### Do I need internet on meet day?

On Scorsync Desktop, no. Judges, the chair, Meet Control and backups only need the laptop and the scoring Wi-Fi; results for scorsync.com wait on the laptop until the internet is back. On Scorsync Cloud, yes: every tablet and the scoring table reach Scorsync over the internet. See [Score with no internet](/offline-host/score-without-internet/).

### Can I run the scoring Wi-Fi from a phone or laptop hotspot?

Not for the pilot: judges' tablets may not find Scorsync at all. Use a travel router. A phone hotspot can give the router its internet, but never turn on a hotspot on the laptop. If you can't get a travel router, contact Scorsync support before the meet. See [Set up Scorsync Desktop and the venue network](/offline-host/set-up-the-venue-network/).

### Is there automatic updating?

No. Scorsync Desktop doesn't update itself. When a new version is out, install it over the old one before meet day, never during it. On Scorsync Cloud there's nothing to install. See [Install a newer version](/offline-host/install-a-newer-version/).

### Do rehearsal scores show anywhere?

No. Practice scores never appear on the public results or on scorsync.com, and **End rehearsal** deletes them. See [Rehearse your meet](/host/before-meet-day/rehearse/).

### Can I undo go-live?

Only before anyone has scored: open the go-live screen again and select **Return to Build**. After the first score, contact Scorsync support (support@scorsync.com). See [Go live](/host/before-meet-day/go-live/#go-back-to-build).

## On the laptop

### Does closing the window stop the meet?

No. Closing or minimising the Scorsync window keeps judges scoring. To open it again, select **Open Scorsync** in the tray menu. **Quit Scorsync**, ⌘Q, or letting the laptop sleep stops scoring for every judge. See [Keep the meet running](/offline-host/keep-the-meet-running/).

Test it at your rehearsal: close the window, and check that a judge can still submit a score.

### Why does it warn "Running without encryption"?

Network diagnostics always shows this on Scorsync Desktop, because tablets talk to the laptop over the scoring Wi-Fi without encryption. It's expected. Keep the scoring Wi-Fi for the laptop and the judges only. See [Check your network before the meet](/offline-host/check-your-network/).

What we tell hosts about scores and names crossing the venue Wi-Fi unencrypted isn't signed off.
:::

### Why doesn't Scorsync Desktop send email?

Scorsync Desktop runs at the venue and never sends email, even with internet. Judges get their sign-in details from printed sheets. If you forget the admin password, contact Scorsync support (support@scorsync.com). See [Print judge sign-in sheets](/host/before-meet-day/print-judge-sign-in-sheets/).

## Sign-in and scoring

### Why can't I sign in with a PIN as a host on Scorsync Cloud?

On Scorsync Cloud, an account that runs meets signs in only with its own email and password. PINs, sign-in QR codes, desk codes and the chair's panel sign-in are for judges. If you also judge, sign in on the tablet with your own password. See [Get judges signed in and scoring](/scoring-table/get-judges-signed-in/).

### Why does only the chair see out-of-range?

The red E-range flag is a warning on the chair's screen; the execution judges' tablets show nothing, so the chair tells them. It doesn't stop the chair moving on. See [Check an out-of-range flag](/judges/chair/out-of-range-flag/).

:::rule[What the rulebook says]
When total E scores are used, the two E scores must be within 0.5 points (0.2 in Double Mini). If they aren't, the chair decides what is best and fairest so the range is met.

**Source:** USA Gymnastics, *Development Program Code of Points: Trampoline* (2025–2029), Section 2, Rule 5.6, page 11, and Rule 2.13, page 8. Double Mini: *Development Program Code of Points: Double Mini* (2025–2029), Section 2, Rule 2.13, page 8, and Rule 5.5.6, page 12.
:::

:::review
Rules statement. The app treats the range as a warning only, while the rulebook says it must be met. Not yet signed off.
:::

## scorsync.com

### Why does the app say "Scorsync Online"?

Scorsync Online is the app's name for scorsync.com, the results site families follow. The "Connect to Scorsync Online" button connects your meet to scorsync.com. See [Connect your meet to scorsync.com](/host/before-meet-day/connect-to-scorsync-com/).

### When do families see scores?

The schedule shows once you lock it. Each score shows once every judge has submitted, or the chair moves on, marked **Unofficial Results**, and turns to **Official Results** when the division is official. For families' own questions, send them to [help.scorsync.com](https://help.scorsync.com). See [Know what families see on scorsync.com](/start-here/what-families-see/).
