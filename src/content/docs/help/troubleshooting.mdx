**Who it's for:** Everyone · Scorsync Cloud and Scorsync Desktop

Find what you see on screen, do the fix, and open the linked page if you need the full steps. For anything not here, contact Scorsync support (support@scorsync.com).

## The tablets are down

- **Every judge's tablet disconnected (lost connection) at once.**
  - **Scorsync Desktop:** check the travel router has power and its Wi-Fi light is on; if it restarted, wait a minute for tablets to rejoin. Then check the Scorsync icon says **● Running**. If not, open Scorsync. See [Keep the meet running](/offline-host/keep-the-meet-running/#reopen-scorsync-after-a-restart).
  - **Scorsync Cloud:** the venue internet is down. Judges' scores wait on their tablets, but the chair can't move on and Meet Control can't save. Restart the router, or switch to your backup internet; judges don't reload. At 30 minutes with no fix in sight, switch to the paper backup sheets. See [Meet-day runbook: Scorsync Cloud](/runbook/scorsync-cloud/#when-something-goes-wrong).
- **Tablets can't score for more than a few minutes.** Switch to the paper score sheets you printed the day before: the panel writes its scores on them, and someone keys them in later. Then contact Scorsync support. See [Print start lists and paper backup sheets](/host/before-meet-day/print-start-lists/#print-the-paper-backup) and [Key scores as a data-entry judge](/scoring-table/key-scores/).

## Tablets and sign-in

- **A tablet can't open Scorsync, or "can't find the server"** (Scorsync Desktop). Check the tablet is on the scoring Wi-Fi, not the venue's. Then, on the host laptop, click the Scorsync icon in the menu bar (Windows: right-click it near the clock), and type the address next to **Or** into the tablet's browser. It's all numbers, like `http://192.168.1.20:8000`. QR codes hold this address too. See [Connect the judges' tablets](/offline-host/connect-judges-tablets/).
- **A tablet can't open Scorsync** (Scorsync Cloud). Check the tablet is on the venue Wi-Fi and can open another website. Then type the address the scoring table gave you exactly, or sign in with a desk code. See [Get judges signed in and scoring](/scoring-table/get-judges-signed-in/#a-desk-code).
- **"Address changed to … — judges are still on …"** in the Scorsync menu, and a yellow banner in the Scorsync window. Another program took the judges' address. Between athletes, select **Restart Server**. If the warning stays, move the judges to the new address, then select **Keep New Address**. See [Keep the meet running](/offline-host/keep-the-meet-running/#if-something-goes-wrong).
- **"Network name is now scorsync-2.local — use Restart Server".** Another Scorsync laptop is on the same Wi-Fi. Select **Quit Scorsync**, then **Quit**, on the other laptop. Then select **Restart Server** on yours when no one is scoring. See [Connect the judges' tablets](/offline-host/connect-judges-tablets/#if-something-goes-wrong).
- **"That code didn't work. Ask for a new one."** The desk code was mistyped, already used or more than 10 minutes old. Select **New code** and try again. See [Get judges signed in and scoring](/scoring-table/get-judges-signed-in/#if-something-goes-wrong).
- **"That email and password or PIN do not match."** Check the email and PIN in **All credentials**, and type them again. If it still fails, use a desk code. See [Get judges signed in and scoring](/scoring-table/get-judges-signed-in/#if-something-goes-wrong).
- **"QR code already used."** Each sign-in QR works once. Select **Sign-in QR** again for a fresh one.
- **"Locked out of sign-in … after too many wrong PINs"** or **"Expired: issue a New PIN or show a Sign-in QR"** in All credentials (Scorsync Cloud). Check it's really the judge, then select **Unlock**, **New PIN** or **Sign-in QR**. See [Show or reset a judge's PIN](/scoring-table/show-or-reset-a-pin/).
- **A host's PIN or desk code is refused, or "This account signs in with its own email and password."** Hosts sign in with their own password. See [the FAQ](/help/faq/#why-cant-i-sign-in-with-a-pin-as-a-host-on-scorsync-cloud).
- **"QR code expired or invalid"** or **"QR code already used"** on a data-entry card. A card's QR code works once, until the date on the card. Sign in with the card's username and password instead. See [Key scores as a data-entry judge](/scoring-table/key-scores/).
- **"This QR code was replaced when the PIN was reset."** The card is out of date: someone selected **New Password** for that account. Use the new card. See [Print judge sign-in sheets and data-entry cards](/host/before-meet-day/print-judge-sign-in-sheets/#if-something-goes-wrong).

## Scoring

- **The session still says "Upcoming", or Run says "The floor is idle".** That's normal: there's no start button. The session starts when the chair opens the first athlete and the first score is in. See [Start a session](/scoring-table/start-a-session/).
- **A judge's tablet doesn't move to the scoring screen.** Ask the judge to select **Go Now** on the countdown, or **Enter Score** on their dashboard. If it says "Waiting for CJP", the chair hasn't opened an athlete yet. See [Start a session](/scoring-table/start-a-session/#if-something-goes-wrong).
- **"My screen jumped to another athlete."** The chair opened the next athlete: tablets show "You're being pulled into scoring" and move by themselves. If the judge was changing a score, they tell the chair the right mark. See [Fix a score you submitted](/judges/fix-a-score/).
- **A score didn't send, or "Score saved on this device".** The tablet lost the Wi-Fi. Keep scoring: the score sends by itself when the Wi-Fi is back. If the tablet shows **Unsent score**, select **Send**. See [Score when the Wi-Fi drops](/judges/score-when-wifi-drops/).
- **"Your saved score … was not applied …"** The chair moved on, or changed the score, before it arrived. Show the chair your paper. See [Score when the Wi-Fi drops](/judges/score-when-wifi-drops/#if-something-goes-wrong).
- **"… It was not applied yet: the chair will accept or refuse it."** The judge's score arrived after the chair moved on. The chair selects **Accept** or **Refuse** under **Late offline scores**. See [Move on when a judge hasn't submitted](/judges/chair/move-on-missing-judge/#if-something-goes-wrong).
- **A judge's card says "Disconnected" on the chair's screen.** That tablet lost the connection and may be holding a score. Ask the judge. See [Score when the Wi-Fi drops](/judges/score-when-wifi-drops/).
- **We can't move on: "Incomplete Submissions".** A judge hasn't submitted. The chair enters the mark from the judge's paper, waits for an offline tablet, or selects **Advance Anyway**. See [Move on when a judge hasn't submitted](/judges/chair/move-on-missing-judge/).
- **A panel is stuck on an athlete, or has gone quiet.** Nudge it, or release it from Run. See [Free a stuck panel or nudge a station](/scoring-table/free-a-stuck-panel/).
- **"Results are official. Ask the meet host to correct a score."** The flight is official, so every scoring button is greyed out. The chair selects **Reopen results**, fixes the score and confirms it official again. See [Reopen an official flight to fix a score](/judges/chair/reopen-official-flight/).
- **"Level 8 maximum DD is 6.0. 6.0 will be stored."** (or another level and number). The difficulty total typed is above the level's maximum. Check it against the paper. If it's right, carry on: Scorsync stores the maximum. See [Get judges signed in and scoring](/scoring-table/get-judges-signed-in/#if-something-goes-wrong).
- **"Not saved — check the slip"** in data entry. A pass is empty, a mark is missing, or a DD is above the maximum. Fix it, or select the save button again to store the scores as entered. See [Key scores as a data-entry judge](/scoring-table/key-scores/#if-something-goes-wrong).
- **"Scores are out of range" when the chair moves on.** It's a warning for the chair only. See [Check an out-of-range flag](/judges/chair/out-of-range-flag/).

## Meet-day changes

- **Meet Builder says "The schedule is locked. Level, birth date, gender, flight and scratch go through Triage."** Don't unlock. Make the change from Run instead. See [Scratch an athlete](/host/meet-day-changes/scratch-an-athlete/) and [Change an athlete's level or flight](/host/meet-day-changes/change-level-or-flight/).
- **A flight isn't in the move list.** Official and finished flights aren't offered. See [Change an athlete's level or flight](/host/meet-day-changes/change-level-or-flight/#move-to-another-flight).
- **"Undo expired. The change is still saved."** Undo lasts about 10 seconds. Change it back the same way, or use **Reinstate**. See [Scratch an athlete](/host/meet-day-changes/scratch-an-athlete/#if-something-goes-wrong).

## Scorsync Desktop laptop

- **There's no Scorsync icon in the menu bar or notification area.** Scorsync isn't open. Open it, and wait for **● Running**. See [Use the Scorsync tray menu](/offline-host/tray-menu/).
- **"Scorsync could not start" with "Ports 80 and 8000–8009 are all in use."** Quit the other programs you don't need, then select **Restart Server** in the tray menu. See [Keep the meet running](/offline-host/keep-the-meet-running/#if-something-goes-wrong).
- **"Scorsync processes from the last run are still running."** Restart the laptop, then open Scorsync again.
- **"Quit Scorsync?"** Select **Cancel** during the meet: Quit disconnects every judge's tablet. Close the window instead. See [Keep the meet running](/offline-host/keep-the-meet-running/).
- **"Backups stopped — a lost database cannot be restored"** across the top of Run. Select **Back up now**. If the line comes back, contact Scorsync support. See [Back up your meet](/offline-host/backups/#if-something-goes-wrong).
- **"Disk almost full — backups and scores may stop saving".** Delete or move large files you don't need, such as videos, then select **Back up now**.
- **"The meet database is missing".** Stop. Don't run setup again, and don't delete anything. Contact Scorsync support (support@scorsync.com): your meet and its backups are still on the laptop.
- **Locked out of the admin account.** Scorsync Desktop can't email you a reset link. Contact Scorsync support (support@scorsync.com). On Scorsync Cloud, select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

## scorsync.com

- **Scores aren't showing on scorsync.com.** A score goes out only once every judge has submitted, or the chair moves on. Then check the Sync Rail chip at the bottom right of Run. See [Check scores are reaching scorsync.com](/scoring-table/watch-publishing/).
- **The chip says "Offline · holding".** Keep scoring: results go out by themselves when the internet is back. To send straight away, select the chip, then **Try now**. See [Score with no internet](/offline-host/score-without-internet/).
- **Families can't find the meet.** The meet isn't connected, publishing is off, or it's still rehearsing: a rehearsal never shows. See [Connect your meet to scorsync.com](/host/before-meet-day/connect-to-scorsync-com/).
- **On Scorsync Cloud, families see "Follow results on Scorsync Online".** That's expected. They select **Go to Scorsync Online** and find your meet. See [Know what families see on scorsync.com](/start-here/what-families-see/).
- **A late entry or schedule change isn't on scorsync.com.** You made it while the schedule was unlocked. [Lock the schedule](/host/before-meet-day/lock-the-schedule/) again.
- **Families ask about their account or a subscription.** Send them to [help.scorsync.com](https://help.scorsync.com).
