Callouts are `:::` blocks. Use them sparingly: one or two on a page. A page full of warnings reads as a page with no warnings.

## Needs Austin's review

Use this for anything Austin must sign off before it ships: T&T rules, scoring math, pricing, refunds, data privacy, and advice that depends on an unsettled decision.

```md
:::review
Hosts bring their own travel router. This follows SCO-2058, which isn't approved yet.
:::
```

:::review
Hosts bring their own travel router. This follows SCO-2058, which isn't approved yet.
:::

You can give it a sharper title:

```md
:::review[Needs Austin's review: pricing]
The pilot is free for the first three meets.
:::
```

:::review[Needs Austin's review: pricing]
The pilot is free for the first three meets.
:::

Remove the callout only once Austin has approved the statement, and mention the approval in the commit message.

## What the rulebook says

Use `:::rule` to quote or paraphrase the USA Gymnastics rulebook. See [Cite the rulebook](/style-guide/rulebook-citations/) for the exact pattern.

## The standard four

| Callout | Use it for | Example |
| -- | -- | -- |
| `:::note` | Extra context the reader can skip. | Rehearsal scores never appear on scorsync.com. |
| `:::tip` | A faster or easier way. | Press ⌘K to find any athlete. |
| `:::caution` | Something that could cost time or need a redo. | Unlocking the schedule lets coaches see changes. |
| `:::danger` | Something that loses scores or stops the meet. | Quit stops scoring for every judge. |

:::note
Rehearsal scores never appear on scorsync.com.
:::

:::tip
Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows) to find any athlete, judge or official.
:::

:::caution
Unlocking the schedule lets you place a late entry, but every change shows on scorsync.com.
:::

:::danger[Don't Quit Scorsync Desktop]
Quit stops scoring for every judge. Close the window instead: the meet keeps running.
:::

These examples show the format only. They haven't been checked against the app.
